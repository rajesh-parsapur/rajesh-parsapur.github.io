Profile

About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Chemistry at IIT Madras. My research focuses on heterogeneous catalysis, hierarchical porous materials, metal–support interfaces, and high-pressure catalytic processes.

My work bridges fundamental catalyst design with practical applications in sustainable fuel synthesis and petrochemical processing, emphasizing structure–performance relationships, selectivity, and catalyst durability.

Focus Areas

Research

We design and investigate functional catalytic materials to understand how pore architecture, interfacial chemistry, and active-site proximity govern reaction pathways.

01 · Sustainable fuels

CO₂ Hydrogenation

Tandem conversion of CO₂ to dimethyl ether and higher-value fuels using multifunctional metal–oxide and acid catalysts.

02 · Catalyst architecture

Confined Catalytic Interfaces

Engineering active sites within hierarchical zeolites to control accessibility, intermediate transfer, selectivity, and long-term stability.

03 · Industrial catalysis

Refinery Applications

Porous catalytic materials and metal–support interfaces for hydroprocessing and selective conversion of complex feedstocks.

04 · Porous materials

Ordered Mesoporous Materials

Tailoring mesoporous silica, carbon, and hybrid frameworks for nanoparticle stabilization and molecular transport.

05 · Valorization

Biomass-Derived Molecules

Selective catalytic hydrogenation of sugars and related oxygenates to useful polyols and chemicals.

06 · Methods

Operando & Reactor Studies

Connecting catalyst structure with performance through high-pressure flow testing, product analysis, and spectroscopy.
